5 Cloud Migration Tips for East African Businesses

June 10, 2026  |  Fatima Zahra  |  5 min read

Migrating to the cloud is one of the most impactful decisions a business can make. For East African companies, the benefits are even more pronounced — reduced infrastructure costs, better disaster recovery, and the ability to scale rapidly.

1. Start with a Cloud Readiness Assessment

Before migrating, understand your current infrastructure, dependencies, and which workloads are cloud‑ready. Han‑Tech offers a free assessment to help you map your migration path.

2. Choose the Right Provider

AWS, Azure, and GCP each have strengths. We recommend a multi‑cloud approach for redundancy and cost optimisation.

3. Prioritise Security and Compliance

Data sovereignty is critical. Ensure your cloud provider complies with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act and other regional regulations.

4. Train Your Team

Cloud migration isn't just a technical shift — it's a cultural one. Invest in training so your team can manage the new environment confidently.

5. Partner with Experts

Han‑Tech has migrated over 50 businesses to the cloud. Let our certified engineers handle the heavy lifting while you focus on your business.
