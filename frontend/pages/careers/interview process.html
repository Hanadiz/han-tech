Journey

Your Interview Journey

A transparent and supportive process from application to offer

1

Application Review

Our HR team reviews your application and CV. We look for skills, experience, and alignment with our values. You'll hear back within 5 business days.

2

Initial Screening Call

A 30-minute phone or video call with our recruiter to discuss your background, career goals, and fit for the role.

3

Technical Assessment

For technical roles, you'll complete a practical test or coding challenge to demonstrate your skills. We provide clear instructions and support.

4

Final Interview

A deeper conversation with the hiring manager and team members. We'll discuss your experience, problem-solving approach, and how you'd contribute to our team.

5

Offer & Onboarding

If successful, you'll receive a written offer. Once accepted, our onboarding team will help you get started and settle into your new role.
