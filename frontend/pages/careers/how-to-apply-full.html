Customize your CV to highlight relevant skills and experience for the specific role.

Write a Cover Letter

🔍

Show that you understand our mission, values, and the work we do.

💪

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the application process

How long does the application process take?

Typically 2-3 weeks from application to offer. We try to move quickly and keep candidates informed throughout.

What should I include in my cover letter?

Highlight your relevant experience, why you're interested in Han-Tech, and how you can contribute to our mission.

Do you accept applications from outside Uganda?

Yes! We welcome applications from qualified candidates worldwide. Remote positions are available.

Will I receive feedback on my application?

We strive to provide feedback to all candidates who reach the interview stage.
