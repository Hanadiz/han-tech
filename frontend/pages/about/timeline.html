Our Journey

Company Timeline

15+ years of growth, innovation, and impact

2010

🏢 Founded

Started in Yumbe, West Nile with 3 engineers and a vision to bridge the digital divide in Northern Uganda.

2013

📈 First Major Client

Signed first government contract, providing IT infrastructure support to district local governments.

2015

🌍 Regional Expansion

Opened offices in Kampala and began serving clients across Uganda, Kenya, and Tanzania.

2018

💡 Innovation Lab

Launched AI and cloud research center, developing custom solutions for agriculture, health, and education sectors.

2020

🚀 100th Client Milestone

Reached 100+ clients and expanded service portfolio to 15 categories with 134+ services.

2022

🏆 Industry Recognition

Won 'Best IT Solutions Provider' award and achieved ISO 27001 certification for information security.

2024

⭐ 500+ Clients

Reached 500+ clients across 4 countries. Launched incubation hub in Yumbe to support local startups.

2026

🎯 The Future

Expanding into new markets, launching AI-powered solutions, and building the next generation of Ugandan tech talent.
