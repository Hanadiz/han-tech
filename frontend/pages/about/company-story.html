Our Story

Founded in 2014 in Yumbe, Uganda, Han-Tech Ltd began as a small IT repair shop. Driven by a passion for technology and a desire to bridge the digital divide in East Africa, we quickly expanded into IT solutions and consulting.

Our journey has been one of continuous innovation – from launching Uganda's first community Starlink hub to deploying enterprise cloud infrastructure for multinational clients.

3+ Years of Excellence

Founded in 2023 with a vision to transform how businesses in East Africa leverage technology.
