Our Story

Founded in 2014 in Yumbe, Uganda, Han-Tech Ltd began as a small IT repair shop. Driven by a passion for technology and a desire to bridge the digital divide in East Africa, we quickly expanded into full‑service IT solutions. Today we are a trusted partner for over 200 businesses, governments, and NGOs across the region.

Our journey has been one of continuous innovation – from launching Uganda’s first community Starlink hub to deploying enterprise cloud infrastructure for multinational clients. Every step forward is guided by our belief that technology, when made accessible, can transform lives and economies.
