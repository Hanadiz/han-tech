Our Story

15+ Years of Excellence

Founded in 2010 in Yumbe, West Nile, Northern Uganda, Han-Tech Ltd started with just three engineers and a vision to transform how businesses in East Africa leverage technology.

From a small team in a rural office to a leading IT solutions provider serving 500+ clients across Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda — our journey has been one of resilience, innovation, and impact.

We bridge the digital divide between urban technology centres and underserved rural communities, delivering 134 professional IT services across 15 categories. Our headquarters remain in Yumbe, strategically positioned to serve the northern frontier and cross‑border markets.
